At some point in life, each of us asks ourselves: "If only I could go back in time and change event X. Then life would surely get better." But few people think about the fact that everything could have turned out completely differently. What are the chances that everything would go exactly as you wanted? All your events and actions in the past have influenced where you are now, what relationships you maintain, what you do, and how you think. Do you really want to go back in time to change something?

Did I want to go back in time? Haha, maybe. But when choosing the moment you want to change, you never know if that choice will turn out to be right. Give someone the opportunity to change their past right here and now - they'll get lost in the ocean of possibilities before them, forgetting about their initial desire. And is it even worth it? We're essentially playing some kind of RPG, rolling the dice every day - will we get lucky or not? By changing something back then, we can't be sure that everything will turn out the way we imagine in our dreams.

Inevitably, sometimes without realizing it, you start thinking about the past, but you have to remind yourself each time - what's gone cannot be brought back. You can only open new branches of development in the present.
