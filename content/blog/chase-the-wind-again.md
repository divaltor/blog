Empty cursor blinking on the white laptop screen. Empty thoughts in my head. A cup of fragrant green tea beside me, filling the room with its delicate aroma. Should I write today? And if yes - then why? I love this process: creating headlines, crafting plots, playing with words... But sometimes there's just endless emptiness in my mind, pulling me in like a black hole.

No one is perfect, and there's no need to chase consistency. It's important to take breaks, to give yourself permission for silence. The best ideas come out of nowhere - exactly when you stop waiting for them.
