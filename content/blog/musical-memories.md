Sometimes I watch or play something and... I hear that very musical accompaniment. I forget about the plot, characters, what's happening around, but the music stays in my head for a long time. For some reason, many people don't even listen to the composition, don't notice its presence in some series or movie, and you can't even share your emotions about the music with anyone. Everyone talks about characters or plot, but have you ever seen anyone say "The composer really created a masterpiece here."

Sometimes I give a 10/10 to a work just because of the music, even if the plot or quality was clearly worse in execution. I love music - it can enhance emotions in a moment to such an extent that this moment will stay in your head forever. I'm sure everyone has that special music that, when listening to it, always brings up a specific memory fragment in your head - that's what I'm talking about.

Listen to music, pay attention to it - it's an essential part of the creation without which it wouldn't be such beautiful.
