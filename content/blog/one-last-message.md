One day you will receive the last message from your friend, meet someone for the last time, laugh at some situation for the last time. Relationships, connections, friendships can break suddenly, especially when you don't expect it. It might be hurtful and sad, but, alas, c'est la vie.

I think the earlier a person faces such experiences, the easier it becomes to handle them in the future. Sometimes it's hard to let someone go, especially if you've become very attached to them: so many shared moments, experiences, and emotions are left behind.

It's easy to write about this, but actually going through... it's hard. But remember, death is not the only thing that can separate you from someone close. The main thing is to find the strength to take the decisive step and move forward, regardless of the past, even if it was beautiful.
