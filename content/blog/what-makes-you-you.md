Who are you? No, I don't mean your name. No, not your profession either. Who are you? Nationality, gender, age, height, IQ? There are thousands of people with similar characteristics. A good parent, reliable friend, loving person? No, that doesn't describe you either. How then do you describe what makes someone truly unique?

People remember each other through actions taken and words spoken. Your thoughts, actions, decisions - that's what makes you unique. A random compliment from a stranger will stay with you for a long time, selfless help from an unknown person at the right moment will be imprinted in your memory for life. From now on, you'll always describe that person by their action, even though you don't know their name - and you don't need to.

So who are you? You are the footprint you leave in other people's lives. The sum of all your actions, decisions, and words. These moments define you as a person, creating an unrepeatable story that no one else can simulate.
