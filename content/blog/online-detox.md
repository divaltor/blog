Sometimes I get this very obsessive idea to give up social media or the internet entirely for a short period of time (a couple of weeks or months) to supposedly "clear my mind" and start everything from scratch. But does it really work if I have to repeat this ritual over and over again from time to time? Maybe the reason lies somewhere else? Perhaps, but I haven't figured out why this happens yet. Maybe I'm desperately trying to convince myself that all my problems stem from spending too much time online, when in reality it's not the case.

Well, never mind. This December, I'll try to spend a month with minimal online presence to gather my thoughts.
