In the modern world, we are surrounded by an endless stream of information - intense work meetings, immersive gaming, continuous learning, and the infinite feed of social media. On busy days, my mind resembles an overflowing flask where thoughts intertwine ceaselessly. In such moments, it feels as if my head might explode from this amount of information.

It's a strange feeling, actually, but I somewhat enjoy it. However, what amazes me even more is what happens when I go for a walk with this "brainfog" head or rid myself of any surrounding noise. As soon as I disconnect, a metamorphosis occurs. The chaotic whirlwind of thoughts gradually subsides, transforming into a light, almost weightless state of emptiness. I love this feeling.
